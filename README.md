# Cartoon Race — GAME RUDRA 🏎️🦖

A cartoon Formula 1 racing game that also teaches times tables. One HTML file,
no installs, no downloads — it runs straight in the browser on a phone, tablet
or computer.

## ▶️ Play it

**https://rmrohira.github.io/Cartoon_Race_GAME_RUDRA/**

Works on iPhone, Android, and desktop. Turn your phone sideways for a
full-width track.

## What's in it

- **Your car is RUDRA** — a hero crest on the engine cover and the name across
  the rear wing.
- **10 cartoon racers** to pick from, every face drawn in code.
- **5 circuits** — Rookie Oval, Harbour Streets, Alpine Pass, Dune Sprint and
  Midnight Circuit.
- **20 laps, 10 chances.** Ten crashes and you retire.
- **Top speed 600 km/h**, and the car stays easy to place up to 350.
- **A dinosaur that pounces clean over the car.** It never actually hits you.
- **Animals that cross the road** and always make it past — no accidents.
- Rain puddles, day/night, an original synthesised soundtrack, and an engine
  note that barks on every upshift.

## The maths 🧮

Trackside billboards are flash cards, three lines at a time. A voice reads out
exactly what is on the board, and everyone claps.

The course runs in a fixed order and **never repeats** until the whole thing is
finished:

1. The **8, 9, 11, 12, 7 and 6** times tables — each one from ×1 to ×12
2. Then **counting on in tens**, starting from 1, then 2, then 3 … up to 9
   (`1 + 10 = 11`, `11 + 10 = 21`, `21 + 10 = 31` … `91 + 10 = 101`)

That's 60 billboards and 162 facts before it starts over.

**Crash into something** and a countdown appears — 10, 9, 8 … 1 — spoken out
loud, for reverse-counting practice. You're back racing in about three seconds.

## Controls

| | |
|---|---|
| Steer | `←` `→` or `A` `D` |
| Throttle | `↑` or `W` |
| Brake | `↓` or `S` |
| Day / night | `N` — switches instantly, even mid-race |
| Mute | `M`, or the speaker button |
| Phone | On-screen steering and pedals appear automatically |

## Notes

The music is an original composition generated in the browser — no licensed
tracks are used, and nothing is downloaded. All characters are original
cartoons; the two lookalike racers have their names shifted by a letter and are
not real people.
